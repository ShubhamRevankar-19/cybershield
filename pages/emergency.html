I Think I Was Scammed

Take a breath. It happens to many people, and acting calmly helps.

Do not use phone numbers or links supplied by the suspicious message itself.
  1. Stop communicating with the suspected scammer.
  2. Do not send additional money or information.
  3. If money or payment details are involved, contact your bank or payment provider through official channels.
  4. Secure affected accounts.
  5. Change compromised passwords.
  6. Report the incident through the appropriate official channel for your country.

For users in India

Cybercrime reporting channels and helplines can change. Please confirm the current official national cybercrime portal and helpline on a government website you reach yourself, or through your bank’s official app, before reporting.
